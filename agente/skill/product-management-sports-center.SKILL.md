This skill ensures the product evolves in the right direction, solving real problems for players and managers while staying competitive.

## Vision & Roadmap

### Strategic Planning
- **Goal Setting**: Define key objectives (e.g., "Reduce manual booking time by 50%").
- **Roadmap Management**: Prioritize features based on impact vs. effort.
- **Competitor Analysis**: Stay ahead of other sports management apps.

### Feature Prioritization
- **Must-Haves**: Core booking, payment, and scheduling.
- **Delighters**: Matchmaking, loyalty rewards, advanced analytics.

## Requirement Definition

### User Stories
- "As a player, I want to see real-time availability so I can book immediately."
- "As a manager, I want an automated revenue report to track daily profits."

### Functional Specs
- Detailed documentation for developers on how features should behave.
- Acceptance criteria for QA to verify feature success.

## Stakeholder Management

- **Facility Owners**: Ensure ROI and operational efficiency.
- **Development Team**: Provide clarity and remove blockers.
- **Users**: Gather and synthesize feedback for continuous improvement.

## Success Metrics (KPIs)

- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of visitors who complete a booking.
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score)**: User satisfaction and likelihood to recommend.
- **Time-to-Value**: How fast a new user completes their first booking.

## DO NOT

- Build features just because "they sound cool" without data backing.
- Ignore technical debt in favor of new features only.
- Lose sight of the core user experience in a feature-rich app.

Lead the product to victory through clear vision and execution.
